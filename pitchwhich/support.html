PitchWhich — Support

A soccer tournament simulator for iPhone and iPad.

Need help?
Email pitchwhich@icloud.com and I'll get back to you. It helps if you say which device and iOS version you're on, and which tournament you were playing.

Common questions

I bought “Remove Ads” but the ads are back.
Open the No Ads tab and tap Restore. Purchases are tied to your Apple Account, so restoring works on any device signed in to the same account. If it still doesn't restore, email me.

Does the app need an internet connection?
No. Every tournament, squad and result is built into the app, and matches are simulated on your device. A connection is only used to show ads and, if you use them, Game Center leaderboards.

Do I need an account?
No. There's no sign-up and no login. Game Center is optional — if you're signed in to it, your best scores can appear on the leaderboards; if you're not, everything else still works.

Where does the data come from?
Real results, goalscorers and squads come from public datasets, including the Fjelstul World Cup Database (© 2023 Joshua C. Fjelstul, Ph.D.), used under CC-BY-SA 4.0. Full credits are in the app under About & Engine.

Why did the favourite lose?
Because that happens. The match engine is calibrated against real results, and a single run is one high-variance sample rather than an average — so underdogs win sometimes, exactly as they do in the real tournament.

I found a bug, or something looks wrong.
Please email me. A screenshot and the year you were playing is usually enough for me to find it.
