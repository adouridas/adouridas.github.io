They're Listening

The game that lets you look behind the curtain.

A game in two halves, forever.

First: four things your phone might think about you. Pick the one you believe it believes. Then the ad it actually served you appears, full screen, and you say whether it got you.

Then it turns around. One clue, four brands, and this time you are the one being tested. That half carries no ads at all.

Two meters keep score. One measures how well they know you. The other measures how well you know them.
